BEFORE YOU REQUEST A DATE

Know what comes next.

Does submitting a request confirm my event?

No. A valid request places your date on hold while the team reviews it. Your dashboard will show the status. The venue team will confirm the arrangements directly with you.

Can the package be tailored to my plans?

Tell us about your guest list, schedule, and priorities in your request. The team will discuss what's possible and confirm the final inclusions and price in your proposal.

Do I pay when I request a date?

No payment is collected on this site. Confirm any deposit, payment schedule, and cancellation terms with the venue team before booking.
