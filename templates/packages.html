Before you request a date

Does submitting a request confirm my event?

No. A request puts your date on hold while the team reviews it. Your account shows the status, and the team confirms the arrangements with you directly.

Can the package be tailored to my plans?

Yes. Describe your guest list, schedule and priorities in your request. The team confirms the final inclusions and price in your proposal.

Do I pay when I request a date?

No payment is collected on this site. Agree any deposit, payment schedule and cancellation terms with the venue team before booking.

Can I change or cancel a request?

Yes. While a request is pending you can change the date and details or cancel it from your account. After it is confirmed, message the team from the event page.
