Can I see the venue before booking?

Yes. Request a walk-through with your preferred date and time window. The venue team will contact you to arrange the visit. Your preferred time is subject to confirmation.

How do I request an event date?

Open event bookings, select an available date, and share your event details. A submitted request puts the date on hold while the team reviews it. It is not a confirmed booking.

How many guests can I invite?

Share your expected guest count and the kind of event you're planning. The booking form accepts requests for 1–200 guests; the team will confirm what works for your layout, seating, and event needs.

What about pricing, catering, and setup?

Ask the team for a quote and current rental details, including catering arrangements, what's included, and setup and cleanup times. These depend on your event; the online request does not take a payment.

Where should guests park? Is there step-free access?

The venue is at 100 S. River Street in downtown Aurora. Contact the team for current parking, drop-off, and accessibility information before your visit, especially if anyone needs step-free access or other accommodations.
